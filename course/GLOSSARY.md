# Model-training course

A professor-led personal course in language-model training, with a core curriculum and student-directed exploration.

## Language

**Progress tracker**:
The learner's private record of tasks, dates, hours, assistance and evidence, kept in `work/admin/` or a tool of their choice; never in the published course.
_Avoid_: Status page in the repo

**Core curriculum**:
The essential learning outcomes that remain required as readings, exercises, projects and scheduling change.
_Avoid_: Fixed reading list

**Milestone**:
A defined learning capability whose achievement is supported by assessment evidence. The instructor designs the milestones; the learner may request additions.
_Avoid_: Deadline, completed reading

**Module**:
A coherent unit of instruction with a defined topic boundary and prerequisites, contributing to one or more milestones.
_Avoid_: Project

**Execution project**:
A bounded piece of learner work with an independently finishable deliverable, contributing evidence toward one or more milestones sized to finish in about three weeks at the learner's chosen pace.
_Avoid_: Entire course

**Assessment**:
A demonstration of correctness, understanding, transfer and experimental judgment, evaluated separately from whether an artifact runs.
_Avoid_: Task completion

**Student idea**:
A learner-proposed question, topic or project whose place in the course has yet to be determined.
_Avoid_: Automatic assignment

**Exploration**:
A bounded investigation driven by a student idea, potentially contributing to an existing milestone or motivating a proposed addition.
_Avoid_: New required milestone

**Equivalent substitution**:
A replacement exercise or project that demonstrates the same required capability and assessment standard within the agreed time and resource limits.
_Avoid_: Skipped outcome

**Milestone defense**:
An independent explanation of a milestone's mechanism and supporting evidence, including an unfamiliar variation and the limits of the conclusion.
_Avoid_: Artifact demonstration alone

**Roadmap**:
The course's learning destinations and their prerequisite relationships.
_Avoid_: Calendar schedule

**Course schedule**:
The learner-chosen allocation of study time and planned dates to instruction, projects, assessment and exploration.
_Avoid_: Hour estimate
